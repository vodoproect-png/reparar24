## Lessons Learned - Desatascos Phase 1

### What Worked Exceptionally Well ✅

1. **Cross-service semantic isolation** - Drainage vocabulary completely separated from plumbing/electrical
2. **Archetype framework scalability** - Works equally well for emergency drainage service
3. **Emergency intent ownership** - 24h urgency positioning established naturally
4. **CCTV/specialist terminology** - Technical drainage vocabulary integrated authentically
5. **Tourism/hospitality emergency focus** - Unique positioning vs residential plumbing

### Drainage-Specific Insights

1. **Grease accumulation angle** - Strong differentiator for restaurant/hospitality districts
2. **Coastal sand/humidity** - Unique drainage challenges in maritime zones
3. **Medieval/historic sewers** - Heritage drainage complexity distinct from modern plumbing
4. **Student misuse scenarios** - Educational opportunity + affordable positioning
5. **Communal bajante emergencies** - Affects multiple units, high urgency, clear positioning

### Process Optimizations

1. **Drainage vs plumbing distinction clear** - Blockages/flow vs leaks/installation
2. **Emergency/24h intent naturally stronger** - Drainage blockages more urgent than general plumbing
3. **CCTV inspection premium angle** - Technology differentiation for commercial/premium
4. **Preventive maintenance upsell** - Natural for drainage (less for reactive plumbing)
5. **Environmental/location factors** - Sand, grease, roots, humidity more relevant for drainage
