## Key Learnings

### What Worked Well

**Technical Specificity:**
- Diameters, pressures, materials = credibility
- Spanish regulations show local expertise
- Brand names (Geberit, Roca) = real business
- Equipment names (termografía, geófonos) = professional

**Real-World Scenarios:**
- "3 de la madrugada" emergency examples
- "desmontar placa frontal sin romper alicatado"
- These show actual job site experience

**Natural Expert Voice:**
- Technical without being academic
- Helpful without being sales-pushy
- "Preferimos prevenir problemas" = trustworthy

### Quality Enhancement Principles

1. **Specificity > Generality**
   - "tubería multicapa Pex-Al-Pex" better than "modern pipes"
   - "cisternas empotradas Geberit" better than "wall cisterns"

2. **Scenarios > Descriptions**
   - "Fugas a las 3 de la madrugada" better than "emergency service"
   - "Coordinamos con alicatadores" better than "full service"

3. **Process > Features**
   - "Localizamos, reparamos, comprobamos" better than "leak detection"
   - "Desmontar placa sin romper alicatado" better than "repair cistern"

4. **Honest > Aggressive**
   - "Si tienes dudas, llámanos" better than "Call now!"
   - "Preferimos prevenir" builds more trust than guarantees
